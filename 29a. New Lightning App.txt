Ah, got it! You want to create an **App** (not an App Page). Here's how:

## **Step-by-Step to Create a Lightning App:**

1. **Go to Setup**
   - Click the **gear icon** (⚙️) in top right
   - Click **Setup**

2. **Open App Manager**
   - In Quick Find box, type: **"App Manager"**
   - Click **App Manager**

3. **Create New Lightning App**
   - Click **New Lightning App** button

4. **App Details**
   - **App Name**: Enter name (e.g., "My Custom App")
   - **Developer Name**: Auto-fills
   - **Description**: Optional
   - **App Image**: Optional
   - Click **Next**

5. **App Options**
   - Choose options (usually keep defaults)
   - Click **Next**

6. **Utility Items** (Optional)
   - Add utility bar items if needed
   - Click **Next**

7. **Navigation Items (Tabs)**
   - Select which tabs to include in your app:
     - Home
     - Accounts
     - Contacts
     - Custom tabs, etc.
   - Click **Next**

8. **User Profiles**
   - Select which user profiles can access this app
   - Click **Save & Finish**

## **Access Your New App:**

- Click **App Launcher** (waffle icon 🧇) in top left
- Find and click your new app name
- Your app will open with the tabs you selected!

**Now you have a custom App!** To add your component to this app, you'd create an App Page (previous steps) and add it as a tab in this app.
