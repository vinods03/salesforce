Note: Classic seems to work better than Lightning here, need to explore Lightning bit more for email templates but used Classic here.

Email Templates: You might want to send mail to thousands of customers. Email Templates help in this.
Setup - Quick find - Email Templates - I chose Classic Email Templates. Lighting Email Templates can also be chosen.
Create a folder (Vinod Email Templates Folder), accessible by all ussers (can be retricted), but no exiting Email template chosen.
In this folder, create a new Email Template
4 types of Email Templates possible:

Text
HTML (using Classic Letterhead)
Custom (without using Classic Letterhead)
Visualforce

``````

Type 1:

Text - Select "Available for use"
Email Template Name: Vinod Text Email Template
Desc: Thank you email template
Sub: Thank you for your inquiry
Email body:

Dear Customer,
Thanks for your interest in our product. We will get in touch with you shortly !!

Best Regards
Customer Care

Using Available Merge fields:
Contact Fields - Name - copy {!Contact.Name}
Sending User Fields - Name - Copy {!User.Name}

The above mail becomes:
Dear {!Contact.Name},
Thanks for your interest in our product. We will get in touch with you shortly !!

Best Regards
{!User.Name}

Switch to Classic View - + tab and Ctrl+F -> Contacts
Select ALL req contacts - choose template - give a name for the mass email - send now or schedule
Check email (For me, it went to Spam)


```````

Type 2:
For this, we first need to create a Document - then Letterhead - finally Email Template
HTML (using Classic Letterhead) - Select "Available for use"

Classic View - + Tab - Ctrl+F -> documents. Create new folder - new document by selecting a jpg (i resized img to 200px * 200px and selected externally available img)
Setup - Quick find - Letterheads - Classic - New - select logo using doc above, change body / footer as needed
Setup - Quick Find - Email Templates - new template using above letterhead
Email Template Name: Vinod Letterhead Email Template
Desc: Thank you email Letterhead template
Sub: Thank you for your inquiry

Email body:
Dear {!Contact.Name},
Thanks for your interest in our product. We will get in touch with you shortly !!

Best Regards
{!User.Name}

Switch to Classic View - + tab and Ctrl+F -> Contacts
Select ALL req contacts - choose template - give a name for the mass email - send now or schedule
Check email (For me, it went to Spam)

Type 3:
Custom (without using Classic Letterhead) - Select "Available for use"
Email Template Name: Vinod Custom Email Template
Desc: Thank you email custom template
Sub: Thank you for your inquiry
Email body:

<pre>
Dear <b>{!Contact.Name}</b>,
Thanks for your interest in our product. We will get in touch with you shortly !!

Best Regards
<i>{!User.Name}</i>
</pre>

Switch to Classic View - + tab and Ctrl+F -> Contacts
Select ALL req contacts - choose template - give a name for the mass email - send now or schedule
Check email (For me, it went to Spam)

Type 4:

Visualforce - Select "Available for use"
Email Template Name: Vinod VisualForce Email Template
Desc: Thank you email visualforce template
Sub: Thank you for your inquiry
 +	 
Email Subject: Thank you
Recipient Type: Contact
Related To Type: Lead

Then Save
Edit Template to:
<messaging:emailTemplate subject="Than you" recipientType="Contact" relatedToType="Lead">
<messaging:plainTextEmailBody >
Hello. Required data is as follows:
Name: {!relatedTo.Name}
Company: {!relatedTo.Company}
Lead Status: {!relatedTo.Status}
</messaging:plainTextEmailBody>
</messaging:emailTemplate>

Send Test and Verify Merge Fields - you need to select a particular contact to send email and a particular Lead about whom to send email.

Note that, this is way of sending more dynamic emails, like a particular object - records level details.
However, you cannot send mass emails (+ tab, Ctrl+F Contacts - Mass Email - this template will not even show up)
