**Public Groups** are manual collections of users that help you share records and manage access more efficiently.

## What Public Groups Are

A public group is a named set of users that can include:
- Individual users
- Roles (and their subordinates)
- Roles and subordinates
- Other public groups (nested groups)
- Queues
- Territories

## How They're Typically Used

### 1. **Sharing Rules**
The most common use - grant access to records based on criteria:

**Example:** "Share all Opportunities in the Western Region with the Western Sales Team group"
- Instead of sharing with 15 individual users, you share with one group
- When new team members join, just add them to the group

### 2. **Manual Sharing**
Quick ad-hoc sharing of individual records:

**Example:** You own an Account and want to share it with the "Product Team" group
- Click Share → Select "Product Team" group → Grant Read/Write access
- All group members can now access that record

### 3. **Folder Access** (Reports, Dashboards, Documents, Email Templates)
Control who can view/edit folders:

**Example:** "Marketing Reports" folder is shared with "Marketing Team" group
- Only Marketing Team members can access these reports

### 4. **Apex Sharing** (Programmatic)
Developers can use groups in code to assign record access dynamically

### 5. **List Email Functionality**
Send mass emails to all group members at once

## Public Groups vs. Other Grouping Mechanisms

| Feature                    | Public Group        | Queue                 | Role |
|---------                   |-------------        |-------                |------|
| **Purpose**                | Flexible sharing    | Workload management   | Hierarchy-based access |
| **Membership**             | Manually defined    | Manually defined      | Org structure |
| **Can own records?**       | No                  | Yes                   | No |
| **Used in sharing rules?** | Yes                 | Yes                   | Yes |

## Typical Real-World Examples

**"Executive Team"** - Give executives access to sensitive accounts/opportunities

**"Finance Department"** - Share financial records across finance users regardless of role hierarchy

**"Regional Sales - Northeast"** - Share regional data with geographically distributed team

**"IT Support Staff"** - Grant access to customer cases or technical records

## Key Advantage

Public groups provide **flexibility** that roles don't - you can group users by function, geography, project, or any business logic without being constrained by the role hierarchy structure.
