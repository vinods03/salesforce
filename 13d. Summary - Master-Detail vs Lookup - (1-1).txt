Master-Detail relationship:
When you delete a record in College table (master), the corresponding records in Student object (child) also get deleted.

Lookup relationship:
When you delete a Staff room (master) record, corresponding Teacher (child) record still exists with blank value in the Staff room field.

Both are one-to-one relationships.

==============

In a Master-Detail relationship, each parent record can have multiple dependent child records linked to it, ensuring that the child records cannot exist without the parent. This structure emphasizes a strong dependency, which is essential for maintaining organized data relationships.

Lookup relationships can also link multiple child records to a single parent, but they do not enforce the dependency or the ownership rules that are characteristic of Master-Detail relationships.
